# How Grizzle Helps Supra Grow

Here is the big picture, in plain words. Each thing Grizzle does has a direct benefit for the Supra ecosystem.

| Area | What Grizzle does | What it means for Supra |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New users | Free-to-start games and guest mode | More people try Supra, and guests become wallet users |
| Wallet activity | Wallet connects, claims and in-game actions | Steady, real on-chain usage |
| SUPRA demand | NFTs, upgrades and mechanics paid in SUPRA | More reasons to hold and use SUPRA |
| Retention | Daily tasks, daily challenge and leaderboard | Players come back every day |
| Community | A growing, active raccoon crew | More people sharing Supra with new audiences |
| Collaboration | Partnerships and shared quests | Other Supra projects get more attention too |
| Proof of what is possible | A live, playable game | A clear example for other builders to follow |

We are not here to pass through the ecosystem. We want to help make it more active, easier to join, and more fun.
