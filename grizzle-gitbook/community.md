# Community and Partnerships

The community is the heart of Grizzle. Here is how you can take part:

* Play the games and climb the leaderboard
* Finish daily tasks and invite your friends
* Join the Telegram community and follow Grizzle on X
* Share feedback that shapes the next update
* Join partner campaigns and quests with other Supra projects

## Working with other Supra projects

We believe Supra grows faster when builders help each other. We are open to partnerships and collaborations such as:

* Joint campaigns and giveaways
* Shared XP quests where players complete tasks for both projects
* In-game integrations
* Community events and AMAs

If you are building on Supra and want to work with us, reach out on X or Telegram. See [Links](links.md).
