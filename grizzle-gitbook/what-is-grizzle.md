# What Is Grizzle

## Vision

To become one of the most fun and active gaming communities on Supra, and an easy first step for people who are new to the ecosystem.

## Mission

* Build games people truly enjoy
* Reward players in SUPRA for their time and skill
* Give the community ownership through NFTs and, later, a token
* Support other Supra builders through partnerships and shared campaigns
* Keep shipping updates and listen to feedback

## The character

Grizzle is a feisty raccoon with a big attitude and even bigger meme power. He lives in the alley, protects the dumpster and never backs down. The mood of the project is playful, loud and community-first. Behind the jokes, the product is built with care.

## Why Supra

Grizzle is mainly on Supra and will keep supporting Supra. Games need to feel quick and smooth, and Supra is a growing ecosystem where an early project can grow together with the chain. We want our players, rewards, NFTs and future token to all strengthen the same network. When Grizzle grows, Supra grows with it.

## Who Grizzle is for

* **Gamers** who want quick, fun games with rewards
* **Meme fans** who love a strong character and a loud community
* **Supra supporters** who want to see more activity on the chain
* **Newcomers** who want an easy, low-pressure first step into crypto
