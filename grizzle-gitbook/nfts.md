# Junkyard Defenders (NFTs)

## What is an NFT?

An NFT is a one-of-a-kind digital item that you own on the blockchain. Because it is recorded on-chain, no one can copy your ownership or take it away. You can keep it, use it, or transfer ownership to someone else.

## The collection

**Grizzle Junkyard Defenders** is the first NFT collection from Grizzle. It has 222 angry raccoons from the junkyard, built for Supra.

* Every Defender is a 1-of-1, so no two are the same
* Each one has its own mix of traits
* The collection is launching on Crystara, a Supra NFT marketplace
* There is a whitelist phase before the public sale
* Purchases are made in SUPRA

## What makes each Defender different

Every Defender wears its own scrapyard gear. Traits include headgear such as spiked helmets, bucket hats, goggles, headsets and crowns, plus outfits like striped shirts, varsity jackets and bandanas, and weapons such as wrenches, crowbars and slingshots. They all stand in a rusty junkyard with the same angry look, which is what makes them Grizzle.

## How the NFTs will be used in the game

The Defenders are meant to be used, not just collected. This is the plan we are building toward, and we will confirm the final details after mint.


1. **Own:** you hold a Defender in your wallet
2. **Play:** your Defender can be your character in Dumpster Defense, with its own look
3. **Build a squad:** your Defender joins your defense team next to Brawlers, Can Tossers, Stink Bombs and Peel Traps
4. **Upgrade:** you can make your character stronger over time, paying in SUPRA and, once it is live, the Grizzle token
5. **Exchange mechanics:** you can use your character to exchange and unlock game mechanics
6. **Trade:** because it is an NFT, you can pass it on through the marketplace to another player

Holders also become part of the core crew, with early access and community benefits to be announced over time.

## How to get a Defender

1. Join the Telegram community and follow Grizzle on X to hear about the whitelist and the launch time
2. Connect your Supra wallet through StarKey and keep some SUPRA ready
3. Go to the Grizzle Junkyard Defenders page on Crystara when minting opens
4. Mint your Defender and see it in your wallet

* Each mint, upgrade and trade is real on-chain activity on Supra
* NFT purchases are paid in SUPRA, which creates demand for the token inside the ecosystem
* A tradable collection brings collectors and traders to Supra marketplaces
* A strong, recognizable collection gets people talking about Supra
