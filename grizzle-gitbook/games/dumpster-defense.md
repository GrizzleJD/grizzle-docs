# Dumpster Defense

Dumpster Defense is the main event. It is a 3D defense game where you protect the dumpster from waves of enemies using a squad of raccoons.

## How it works

* You build a defense squad of up to 8 raccoons
* Enemies come in waves, and your squad has to hold the alley
* Each round the waves get faster, so the game gets harder the longer you last
* Every point you score becomes 1 XP, with a maximum of 4,000 XP per game

## Your defenders

* 🥊 **Brawlers:** close-range fighters who stand at the front and take on enemies head-on
* 🥫 **Can Tossers:** raccoons who throw cans at enemies from a distance
* 💨 **Stink Bombs:** smelly gas attacks that hit groups of enemies
* 🍌 **Peel Traps:** banana peels placed on the ground to trip up enemies

{% hint style="success" %}
**A simple tip:** mix different defender types. A squad with a bit of everything is stronger than a squad with only one kind.
{% endhint %}
