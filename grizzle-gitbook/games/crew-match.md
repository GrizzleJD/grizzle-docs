# Crew Match

Crew Match is a memory card game for players who prefer thinking to fast tapping.

## How it works

* There are four levels
* Every time you play, the cards are different
* As you move up, you get less time to peek at the cards
* Clear all four levels to earn 1,500 XP

## Why it is fun

It is calm, it trains your memory, and the shrinking peek time makes each level a fresh challenge.
