# Trash Bash

Trash Bash is a fast arcade game, and the easiest one to start with.

## How it works

* Grab the mallet
* Raiders pop out of the trash cans, and you whack them back down
* You earn 10 XP for every point, with a maximum of 1,500 XP per game
* Each level is faster than the last, so you need quick reactions

## Why it is fun

Sessions are short, the rules are simple, and you see your score go up right away. It is perfect for a quick break.
