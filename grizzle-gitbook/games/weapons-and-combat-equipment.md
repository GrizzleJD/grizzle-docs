### Combat equipment and Junkyard Defense

Junkyard Defense revolves around protecting the dumpster from incoming enemies.

\
Weapon effectiveness is an important part of combat strategy. Players should consider weapon statistics when selecting their equipment and preparing their Defenders.

\
The Grizzle Junkyard Defenders NFT collection also adds utility to the gameplay experience. Owning and deploying the complete NFT set can make defending the dumpster easier.

### Equipment categories



* Melee: Close-range equipment such as axes, clubs, flails, sickles and crowbars.
* Ranged: Equipment designed for attacks from a distance, such as the slingshot.
* Utility: Items that provide tactical or environmental advantages, such as the banana peel and mechanical grabber.
* Special combat equipment: Items with potential unique effects, such as the bomb, electric device and flame brush
