# Grizzle Arena

## How it works

1. Connect your StarKey wallet on the site
2. Your Grizzle NFTs show up automatically once your wallet is connected
3. Choose 3 Grizzle NFTs to send into battle
4. Each NFT fights with its own weapon and level
5. Battle another player in a 3v3 match
6. Win, and earn 10,000 XP

### Practice first

Not ready to fight a real player? Warm up against the Junkyard Bot. It is for practice only and pays no XP, so you can learn how your NFTs fight without any pressure.

### Why your NFT's weapon matters

Every Grizzle NFT carries its own weapon and has its own level. That is what makes each fighter different in battle, so the three you pick matter. Think about your squad before each match: choose NFTs whose weapons work well together.
