# Introduction

## Why we built Grizzle

Most people do not find a blockchain by reading technical papers. They find it through something fun: a game, a meme or a friend's invite. Games and memes are easy to understand, easy to share and easy to repeat, which is why they work so well as a way in.

## The problem we saw

* Crypto can feel confusing to newcomers, with wallets, tokens and new tools to learn all at once
* Many play-to-earn projects promise a lot but have no game to play at launch
* Ecosystems need activity, and activity only comes from products people actually enjoy using

## Our answer

We built the product first. The website and games are live today, so anyone can try them right now. A new player can join as a guest with only a nickname, earn XP, and connect a Supra wallet later when they feel ready. Each step is short, fun and rewarding, so the first experience never feels like homework.

## What this means for Supra

Every new player is a possible new wallet user. Every wallet connected, reward earned and NFT collected is real activity on Supra. Grizzle is built to create that activity in a friendly way.
