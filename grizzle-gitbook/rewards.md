# The Reward System

The reward system has three simple steps: you play, you earn XP, and your XP turns into SUPRA. This page explains each step, the daily tasks, and the rules that keep things fair.

## What is XP?

XP stands for experience points. It is how Grizzle keeps score of everything you do. You earn XP by playing the games and by finishing daily tasks. The rewards page shows your lifetime XP, your XP today and your claimable SUPRA, all updated in real time.

## How XP turns into SUPRA

Your XP can be converted into SUPRA. As your XP grows, the rewards page shows how much SUPRA you can claim. At the time of writing, the page shows about 1 SUPRA for every 1,000 XP. This rate may change as the project grows.

* You can already see your claimable SUPRA on the rewards page
* Claiming opens after launch
* Every claim is reviewed by the team
* Payouts are sent in batches, not one by one

The review step protects the reward pool from cheating, so honest players get their fair share.

## Daily tasks

Tasks are small actions that give you extra XP. They also help new players learn how everything works.

| Task | What you do | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connect Supra wallet | Link your wallet to your account | +1,000 XP |
| Join Telegram | Join the Grizzle community chat | +1,000 XP |
| Follow Grizzle on X | Follow the official account | +1,000 XP |
| Complete profile | Fill in your player profile | +1,000 XP |
| Invite a friend | Share your invite link | +1,000 XP when a friend joins, then +2,000 XP more when they connect a wallet |

## The Daily Challenge

The Daily Challenge is the biggest single reward. Play 3 of the 3 games in a day and claim **+5,000 XP**. It resets every day at 8:00 AM Philippine time, so there is a fresh chance to earn it each morning.

## Fair play rules

* **Server-checked XP:** our server verifies your XP, so it cannot be faked by editing the game on your device
* **Daily cap:** each player can earn up to 100,000 XP per day, so no one can drain the reward pool in a single day
* **Reviewed claims:** the team reviews every claim before payout
* **Batch payouts:** rewards are sent in groups, which keeps the process orderly

## Guest or wallet: your choice

You do not need a wallet to start playing.

| | Guest | Wallet player |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How to join | Pick a nickname | Connect a Supra wallet through StarKey |
| Can play and earn XP | Yes | Yes |
| Keeps XP for claiming | Connect a wallet before launch to keep it | Yes |
| Can disconnect | Not needed | Yes, at any time |

Practice runs need neither a nickname nor a wallet, so anyone can try the games first.

## Leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks players by XP. Top players get recognition from the community, so there is a friendly race to the top every week.

## Built from your feedback

We update Grizzle based on what players tell us. Recent updates include wallet connect, sound, a disconnect option and clearer, easier-to-read fonts. If something feels off or something is missing, tell us in the community chat and we will listen.
