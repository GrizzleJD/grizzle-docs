# Security and Fairness

We want players to trust Grizzle. These are the rules we follow:

* **Server-checked XP:** progress is verified by our server
* **Daily caps:** a limit of 100,000 XP per player per day
* **Reviewed payouts:** claims are reviewed by the team and sent in batches
* **You control your wallet:** you connect and disconnect your own wallet whenever you like
* **Open communication:** any change to rewards, NFTs or the token is announced to the community
