# FAQ

## Do I need a wallet to play?

No. You can join as a guest with a nickname, or try practice runs with no account at all. Connect a Supra wallet before launch to keep and claim your XP.

## Which wallet do I use?

Grizzle supports Supra wallet connection through StarKey.

## How do I earn the most XP?

Play all three games, finish the daily tasks, complete the Daily Challenge and invite friends.

## What is the Daily Challenge?

Play 3 of the 3 games in one day to earn +5,000 XP. It resets every day at 8:00 AM Philippine time.

## When can I claim SUPRA?

Claiming opens after launch, and every claim is reviewed by the team. You can see your claimable balance on the rewards page at any time.

## Is there a limit to how much XP I can earn?

Yes. The limit is 100,000 XP per player per day.

## Is the Grizzle token live?

Not yet. We are focusing on the product first and looking at launchpad options for an ITO.

## How many Defender NFTs are there?

222. Each one is a 1-of-1 with its own traits, launching on Crystara.

## How will the NFTs be used?

As characters in Dumpster Defense that you can upgrade, use to exchange mechanics, and pass on through the marketplace. See [Junkyard Defenders (NFTs)](nfts.md) for the full plan.

## Can other projects work with Grizzle?

Yes. We welcome partnerships and collaborations. Contact us on X or Telegram.
