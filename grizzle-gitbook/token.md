# The Grizzle Token

## Where things stand

The Grizzle token is **not live yet**. We are not in a rush. Our first priority is to make the product the best it can be. Today, rewards and NFT purchases use SUPRA.

## What the token is planned to do

Once it is live, the token will work together with the games. Along with SUPRA, it is meant to power the upgraded characters and the in-game economy, for things like exchanging mechanics and upgrading your character.

## SUPRA and the Grizzle token, side by side

| | SUPRA | Grizzle token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | Live now | Not live yet |
| Rewards | Players earn XP that converts to SUPRA | Planned for the future |
| NFTs | Used to buy Defenders | Planned to help with upgrades |
| Game economy | Buy, upgrade and trade mechanics in SUPRA | Planned to work alongside SUPRA |

## How we plan to launch

We are looking at the best launchpad options to open our token launch (TGE) through an ITO. We have not picked one yet. We would rather choose carefully than rush.

## What you can expect from us

Before launch, we will share the full details with the community first. That includes how many tokens there will be, who receives them, when they unlock, and what they are used for.
