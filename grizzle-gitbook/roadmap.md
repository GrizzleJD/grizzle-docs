# Roadmap

Our roadmap is a list of phases, not a list of dates. We would rather ship things well than hit a deadline and ship them badly. The order and details may change as we learn from the community. Under each phase, we explain how it helps Supra grow.

## Phase 1: Foundation (live now)

**What is happening**

* Website and three games: Dumpster Defense, Trash Bash and Crew Match
* XP system with server checks and a daily cap
* Guest mode and Supra wallet connect through StarKey
* Daily tasks, Daily Challenge and leaderboard
* Updates from community feedback: sound, disconnect and clearer fonts
* Partnerships with fellow Supra projects, including SoupOnSupra, suprabag and Rez Dawgz

**In simple words:** the game is built and open to play.

**How it helps Supra:** a working product that welcomes new users, creates new wallet connections, and gives people a fun reason to try Supra on day one.

## Phase 2: NFT launch

**What is happening**

* Whitelist phase for the early community
* Public mint of Grizzle Junkyard Defenders (222 pieces) on Crystara
* Community campaigns and partner collaborations around the launch

**In simple words:** the community gets its characters.

**How it helps Supra:** mints paid in SUPRA, new visitors to Supra marketplaces, and a recognizable collection that carries Supra's name.

## Phase 3: Rewards and Defender integration

**What is happening**

* Reward claiming opens, with team review and batch payouts
* Defenders start to appear in Dumpster Defense as playable characters
* Upgrades and character features are added step by step

**In simple words:** your XP turns into SUPRA, and your NFT starts to matter in the game.

**How it helps Supra:** real SUPRA reaches players, more wallets hold and use SUPRA, and NFT holders actively use their assets on Supra.

## Phase 4: Token and TGE

**What is happening**

* We choose the launchpad route for the ITO
* We publish the full token details, including supply and allocation
* The token joins the game for upgrades and mechanics, alongside SUPRA

**In simple words:** the Grizzle token goes live and becomes part of the game.

**How it helps Supra:** a new community token launched inside the ecosystem, bringing new holders, trading activity and liquidity to Supra.

## Phase 5: Marketplace and expansion

**What is happening**

* Marketplace flows where mechanics and characters are bought, upgraded and passed on in SUPRA
* More games added to Grizzle, all centered on Supra
* Bigger partnerships, tournaments and joint campaigns with other Supra projects

**In simple words:** a full gaming world with more games and a living economy.

**How it helps Supra:** a self-sustaining gaming economy, a steady stream of new games, and a regular flow of fresh users for the whole ecosystem.
